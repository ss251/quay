Deploy a folder to NEAR. Move production when you mean to.

Every deploy is sealed as a release with its own URL. Production is a pointer: promote a release, or roll back to the last one. A publish-only key can upload a preview and cannot touch production.

open the dashboard read the docs

Storage costs about 1 NEAR per 100 KB and stays locked until you delete the release. Testnet only.
