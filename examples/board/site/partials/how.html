How this page works

  • Release: these files and routes are sealed in release {{site.release}} on {{site.account}}.
  • Database: each notice is a document in the posts collection, read live.
  • Routes: the contract fills a template with the documents and answers this request itself.
  • Members: joining creates name.{{site.account}} through a relayer, with no NEAR needed.
  • Functions: a moderation function checks each new post and builds its link preview.
  • Payments: a wallet can pay to pin a notice for 24 hours.
  • Cron: an hourly job writes the harbour digest.
  • Large files: the picture above is stored off chain and checked by hash.
