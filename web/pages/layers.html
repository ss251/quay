Layers

A site on one NEAR testnet account can hold its pages, its data, its sign-in, its functions and its scheduled work. Each layer has a page with its commands, how Harbour Board uses it, proof on testnet, what you trust and its limits.

Status on 2026-11-11

Every layer below was run on testnet on one sample, Harbour Board, account harbour-board.testnet, contract 3.0.1. This site and the static sample, quay-esc0v902.testnet and quay-65nqd3rn.testnet, still run 2.0.0, which has the Site and Gateway layers only.
